🧪How to test

Three quick things. About five minutes in total.

  1. 🔕

    Step 1

    Flip to silent, then tap

    Tap stickers in the app and in the Messages drawer. Each one should play.

  2. ⏱️

    Step 2

    Leave for a minute

    Switch to another app, come back and tap a sticker straight away.

  3. 🎙️

    Step 3

    Record, play, send

    Record a sound and play it right away, then send a new sticker from the drawer.

💬Tell us what you find
