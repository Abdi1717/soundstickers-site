Help test Sound Stickers

Stickers you can hear, right in Messages. Here’s how to install the beta, what to try, and how to tell us what you find. It takes about 10 minutes.

Get the beta in TestFlight 🎉 What’s new in build 8

1Install the beta

  1. Get TestFlight from the App Store. It’s free and made by Apple.
  2. On your iPhone, tap the invite link: testflight.apple.com/join/r7hY3KT8
  3. Tap Accept, then Install. Open Sound Stickers once to finish setup.
Open the invite link

2Find it in Messages

Sound Stickers lives inside Messages, in the + menu.

Tip: touch and hold Sound Stickers and drag it to the top so it’s always one tap away.

  1. 1. Open any chat

  2. 2. Tap +

  3. 3. Scroll down, tap Sound Stickers

3What to try

Five quick things. Each takes under two minutes.

  1. Try 1

    Play a starter sticker

    Open a starter pack and tap a sticker to hear it.

  2. Try 2

    Make your own

    Record a sound, pick art, add Splat + Echo, then save.

  3. Try 3

    Send one in Messages

    Open a chat, tap + › Sound Stickers, tap a sticker, send.

  4. Try 4

    Send with a Screen effect

    Pick the 🍅 tomato and send it with the Echo screen effect.

  5. Try 5

    Check where it’s stored

    Settings › iCloud & Privacy should say everything is on this iPhone.

4Send feedback

Anything odd, confusing or great: we want to hear it.

  • In the app

    Settings › Help › Send Feedback (coming in build 3). It opens Mail with your note and (if you like) your iPhone model, iOS and app version. Until then, email abdimohamed1717+soundstickers@gmail.com.

  • With a screenshot

    Take a screenshot, tap the thumbnail, then tap Share Beta Feedback. TestFlight sends it to us.

  • If it crashes

    TestFlight asks if you want to send a crash report. Say yes, and add a line about what you were doing.

5Good to know

  • iPhone only for now (no iPad or Mac).
  • Needs iOS 18 or later.
  • The people you send to don’t need the app. They get a video with sound.
