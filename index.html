How it works

Your sounds, as stickers

Your friend's laugh, a perfectly timed air horn, the splat of a tomato. Make it once and it's in your Messages drawer for good.

Make one in seconds

Hold to record up to 10 seconds, or pull the sound out of a video in Photos or a file in Files. Trim to the exact moment.

Effects everyone sees

Splat, Echo, Slam, Shake, Confetti, Hearts and more. Stack two, like Splat + Echo. The effect is part of the sticker, so it plays for everyone.

Right in Messages

Open any chat, tap +, then Sound Stickers. Your favorites, recents and packs are all there. Tap one to place it, then send.

Everyone hears it

Friends without the app get a short video of the art and the sound. They tap it and hear it. Captions help when phones are on silent.

Send with Effect

Throw a tomato with Echo

Pair your sticker with an iMessage screen effect. Echo fills their screen with tomatoes, and they tap to hear the splat.

  1. Tap a sticker in the drawer to place it.
  2. Touch and hold the blue Send arrow ↑.
  3. Tap Screen, swipe to Echo, then send.

Getting started

How to find it in Messages

On iOS 26, new iMessage apps are added at the bottom of the + list. Move Sound Stickers to the top once and it's always one tap away.

On iOS 18, tap +, then More, to find Sound Stickers. Stuck? See Support.

Private by design

Your sounds stay on your iPhone

There's no Sound Stickers server, so there's nothing to upload your recordings to.

  • No account needed. Sign in with Apple is optional.
  • No ads, no tracking. The app collects no data.
  • Captions on device. Speech is turned into captions on your iPhone.
  • Yours to remove. Export your stickers or delete everything from the app.

Read the Privacy Policy. This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers either.
