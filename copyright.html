How Sound Stickers works

Sound Stickers is a tool. Everything you make stays on your iPhone and is sent through Apple Messages. We don't host, store, index or distribute anyone's stickers, so we can't see, remove or take down a sticker someone made or sent.

Make stickers you have the right to make

Use sounds and images you created or have permission to use. Don't use the app to make or send:

  • Copyrighted material you don't have rights to, such as songs, film, TV or game audio, podcasts, or artwork. Short clips aren't automatically "fair use".
  • Recordings of people who didn't agree to be recorded. In many places, recording a private conversation without everyone's consent is illegal.
  • Someone's voice, name or face used to mislead, mock, harass or advertise, or deepfake-style impersonation.
  • Trademarks and logos used in a way that suggests a brand made or endorses your sticker.
  • Illegal content, threats, harassment or bullying, hate, sexual content involving minors, or intimate images shared without consent.

The app never downloads audio from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or other services. It only imports files that are already on your device.

Built-in content

The built-in sounds are original synthesized sounds made for Sound Stickers. The starter-pack emoji art is based on Noto Color Emoji © Google LLC (SIL Open Font License 1.1 / Apache License 2.0); see Settings → About → Acknowledgements in the app. If you believe any built-in content infringes your rights, contact us and we'll investigate and, if needed, remove it in the next update.

If someone sent you a sticker that infringes your rights or harasses you

  • In Messages, report it or block the sender (Apple: block, filter and report messages).
  • Contact the person who sent it, or the platform where it was posted.
  • We can't remove it, because we never receive or host it.
