Privacy Policy

Sound Stickers for iPhone and iMessage · Last updated October 18, 2026

The short version

Your sounds stay on your iPhone. Sound Stickers has no servers that store your content, and we don't collect, sell or share any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Stickers, sounds, artwork and the videos the app makes are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone, and shared with the Sound Stickers iMessage extension on the same device.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional). If you sign in, the app keeps your Apple user identifier and the name you choose in your iPhone's Keychain. It is never sent to us. You can use the app without an account.
  • Microphone is used only while you record a sound.
  • Speech recognition runs on your device to suggest captions. Audio isn't sent to us.
  • Photos and Files are accessed only for the items you pick to import.

What leaves your device

Only what you choose to send. When you send a sound sticker in Messages, it's sent as a video message through Apple's Messages app, like any other attachment. Your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) includes the app's data, under Apple's own privacy terms.

Your controls

In the app, go to You → Account. Export My Stickers saves a copy of everything you've made. Delete All Data on This iPhone permanently removes all of it. Deleting the app also removes its data.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
