Privacy Policy

Sound Stickers for iPhone and iMessage · Effective October 19, 2026

The short version

Your sounds stay on your iPhone. Sound Stickers has no servers that store your content and no accounts on our side. We don't collect, sell or share any personal information. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Who we are

Sound Stickers is made by Abdinajib Hussein Mohamed, an independent developer in the United States ("we"). Because the app doesn't send us any personal information, we don't hold any data about you.

What the app uses, and where it stays

Everything below is processed on your iPhone and is never sent to us.

  • Stickers, sounds, artwork, captions and the videos the app makes are stored in the app's storage on your iPhone and shared with the Sound Stickers iMessage extension on the same device.
  • Microphone is used only while you hold the record button.
  • Speech recognition (optional) turns your clips into suggested captions entirely on your iPhone. The app requires on-device recognition; if your iPhone can't do it on-device, captions are skipped and nothing is sent to Apple or us.
  • Photos and Files: the app reads only the items you pick to import. Subject cut-out for photo art runs on your device.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional). If you sign in, the app keeps your Apple user identifier and the name you choose in your iPhone's Keychain to personalise the app. We never receive it. You can use the app without an account.
  • Settings and preferences (like haptics or send mode) are stored on your device.

What leaves your device

Only what you choose to send or back up:

  • Messages. When you send a sound sticker, it goes as a video through Apple's Messages app (or wherever you share it), like any other attachment, under Apple's or that service's privacy terms. We can't see or delete sent stickers.
  • iPhone backup. Your iCloud or computer backup includes the app's data, under Apple's privacy terms.
  • Export. If you use Export My Stickers, the file goes wherever you save or share it.

Apple services you use

Messages, Sign in with Apple, iCloud Backup and the App Store are provided by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We don't receive data from them. App Store download and crash statistics that Apple shares with developers are anonymous and aggregated, and are only shared if you opted in to share them with developers.

We don't sell or share personal information

We don't sell, rent or share personal information, and we don't use it for targeted advertising. We have none to sell.

How long data is kept

Your content stays on your iPhone until you delete it. Deleting a sticker removes it from the app. Delete All Data on This iPhone (You → Account) permanently removes every sticker, pack, recording and your profile, and clears the sign-in identifier from the Keychain. Deleting the app removes its stored stickers. iOS may keep the Keychain sign-in identifier after the app is deleted, so sign out or use Delete All Data first if you want it gone. Copies in older backups follow your backup settings.

Your choices

  • Use the app without signing in, and skip any permission.
  • Turn off microphone, speech recognition or photos access at any time in iOS Settings → Apps → Sound Stickers.
  • Sign Out (You → Account) removes your sign-in from this iPhone. To disconnect Sound Stickers from your Apple Account, go to iOS Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → Sound Stickers → Stop Using Apple Account.
  • Export My Stickers saves a copy; Delete All Data on This iPhone erases everything.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or the UK), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Since we don't collect or hold personal information, there's nothing for us to access or delete, but you control everything in the app as described above. You can still contact us with any request and we'll answer.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Our Terms of Use ask that children under 13 use the app only with a parent's or guardian's permission. A parent with questions can contact us as below.

Our website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We use no cookies, analytics or trackers, but GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and secure the service (GitHub Privacy Statement).

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version on this page with a new effective date, and tell you in the app if the change is significant. If we ever start collecting data, we'll update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that change ships.
