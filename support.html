Support

Find Sound Stickers in Messages

Open a chat and tap +. Scroll the list: new apps appear at the bottom. Touch and hold Sound Stickers and drag it to the top. Next time it's the first thing you see after tapping +.

Send with an effect

Tap a sticker so it's placed in the message, then touch and hold the blue Send arrow, pick Bubble or Screen, and choose an effect such as Slam or Echo.

I don't hear the sound

Previews follow your iPhone's silent switch. Tap the speaker button to play anyway, or turn on "Play previews on silent" in You → Haptics & Sound.

Back up or remove my stickers

You → Account → Export My Stickers, or Delete All Data on This iPhone.
